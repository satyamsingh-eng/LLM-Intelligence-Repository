    SALES ENABLEMENT & CLIENT PITCH GUIDE How to Pitch SARVAX Architecture to Non-Technical Prospects & Wealth CXOs
    Sales Team Playbook · Non-Technical Primer
    THE 3-LAYER PITCH Productivity vs. Agentic vs. Platform AI

    When pitching to a CEO or Head of Wealth, divide SARVAX into 3 simple layers:

    • Layer 1 (Productivity): What the advisor sees — audio transcription, WhatsApp OCR, auto-filling CRM tasks.
    • Layer 2 (Agentic): Bounded specialized agents (Pre-Meeting Agent, Portfolio Analyst) that ingest data and write drafts.
    • Layer 3 (Platform & Guardrails): Workflows 2.0 DAG engine enforces exact Decimal math, policy checks, and mandatory advisor sign-off.
    TOKEN ECONOMICS ELI5 The "Reading vs. Writing" Analogy

    Explain why input costs dominate output costs in financial advisory:

    • Reading (Input): The AI reads 50 pages of portfolio statements + 3 years of CRM history (75,000 tokens). Reading is cheap (₹14.50–₹29.00/1M).
    • Writing (Output): The AI writes a concise 2-page rebalancing summary (8,000 tokens). Writing is 5x costlier due to sequential GPU generation.
    • Key Takeaway: Reading volume is 10x higher, making input tokens the main driver of overall run costs.
    COST SAVINGS CHEAT-SHEET How Prompt Caching Slashes Client Invoices

    Use these exact numbers during commercial pricing calls with prospects:

    • Uncached Standard Rate: Portfolio review = ₹29.69 / run (₹2.97 Lakhs/mo for 10k runs).
    • With 80% Prompt Caching: Cost drops to ₹10.97 / run (₹1.10 Lakhs/mo) — 63% client savings!
    • WhatsApp Automation (NS Wealth): Cost is just ₹0.24 / check (only ₹57.60/client/month for 24/7 coverage).
    OBJECTION HANDLING PLAYBOOK Answering CXO Security & Accuracy Questions

    Two core objections every wealth manager asks during a demo:

    • "Will the AI hallucinate portfolio returns?" — Answer: No. In SARVAX, models never do math. Our Python Decimal engine calculates 100% exact return numbers.
    • "Can AI send emails without approval?" — Answer: No. Every outbound action hits an await_approval gate requiring human advisor click-to-confirm.

    Enterprise Governance, SEBI & Global Compliance

    SEBI Investment Adviser Circulars, FINRA Rule 3110 / 2010, SEC Rule 204-2, EU AI Act Annex III / Article 15, SOC 2 Type II, and FedRAMP High define distinct governance boundaries. This section separates legal scope, attestations, audit logs, and SARVAX engineering precision policy.

    SEBI IA Regulations

    SEBI Investment Adviser & Advisory Compliance

    Under SEBI (Investment Advisers) Regulations, 2013 and SEBI Cybersecurity Framework, AI systems generating portfolio advice or mutual fund recommendations must maintain deterministic suitability checks, risk-profiling validation, and mandatory advisor sign-off before client dispatch. Models cannot directly execute transactions without human authorization.

    SEBI IA Regulations
    FINRA & SEC Audit Trails

    FINRA Rule 3110 / 2010 & SEC Rule 204-2

    FINRA Rule 3110 (Supervision) and SEC Rule 204-2 (Books and Records) require complete immutability and 7-year retention for all AI-generated client communications, meeting notes, and suitability logs. SARVAX enforces structured JSON schema audit logging with hash signatures for every agent call.

    FINRA Rule 3110
    SARVAX Indian Wealth Advisory Compliance Pack
    • SEBI Risk-Profiling & Asset Allocation Suitability Gate prior to recommendation generation.
    • Cryptographic WORM (Write Once Read Many) log storage for SEC 17a-4 compliance.
    • Section 195 TDS & Capital Gains Indexation calculation engine decoupled outside LLMs.
    • Human-in-the-Loop (HITL) advisor verification workflow with explicit digital sign-off.
    Verified legal text

    Annex III applicability is use-case specific

    Annex III includes AI used to evaluate the creditworthiness or credit score of natural persons and AI used for risk assessment and pricing for natural persons in life and health insurance. A portfolio-analysis or wealth-advisory system is not automatically Annex III high-risk solely because it operates in finance; intended purpose and material influence must be assessed.

    Official EU Annex III
    Verified legal text

    Article 15: accuracy, robustness and cybersecurity

    Article 15 requires high-risk AI systems to achieve an appropriate level of accuracy, robustness and cybersecurity throughout their lifecycle, declare relevant accuracy metrics and be resilient to errors, faults and attacks. It does not name INT4, FP8 or BF16.

    European Commission Article 15
    SARVAX evidence required before EU deployment
    • Document intended purpose, affected persons and Annex III applicability.
    • Define task-specific accuracy, calibration, numeric error and robustness thresholds.
    • Retain validation datasets, versioned test results, human oversight and incident controls.
    • Map provider, deployer and importer responsibilities before claiming conformity.
    Third-party attestation

    SOC 2 Type II

    SOC 2 Type II concerns controls for a defined service-organization system over a review period. It is not a compliance badge inherited by an individual model. Review the report scope, Trust Services Criteria, examination period, exceptions and bridge coverage.

    Where PHI is handled, a covered entity must obtain appropriate assurances through a business-associate contract and confirm the relevant service and data flow are in scope. “HIPAA eligible” model availability alone is insufficient.

    Private VPC is not automatic air-gapping

    Self-hosting a model in a VPC does not by itself guarantee air-gapping or “100% cloud data sovereignty.” Sovereignty depends on region, identity, egress, private endpoints, encryption keys, logs, backups, telemetry, support access, subprocessors and the model-artifact supply chain.

    FedRAMP High review boundary
    • Named service and FedRAMP Marketplace status.
    • Authorization boundary, region and agency authorization path.
    • Inherited controls versus SARVAX/customer-responsible controls.
    • Model endpoint, data stores, logging, networking and operations inside the boundary.
    Not statutory text

    Article 15 does not prohibit INT4

    The statement “INT4 is strictly prohibited and FP8/BF16 are required” is not present in Article 15. Quantization can affect numeric fidelity, but the legal requirement is outcome-based accuracy, robustness and cybersecurity.

    Verify Article 15
    SARVAX engineering policy

    Material financial-risk scoring precision gate

    SARVAX policy: do not deploy INT4 for material financial-risk scoring unless model-specific validation demonstrates no material degradation against the approved reference precision. FP8/BF16 may be approved only after the same tests; neither is automatically safe.

    Required precision-validation pack
    • Exact model/version, quantization method and reference precision.
    • Representative financial dataset and materiality threshold.
    • Numeric error, ranking/discrimination, calibration and tail-risk results.
    • Adversarial robustness, drift monitoring, sign-off owner and residual risk.
