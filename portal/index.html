EU AI Act (2024/1689) Financial Controls

Financial services workloads (credit scoring, risk analysis, KYC) fall under Annex III High-Risk AI. Mandatory obligations:

  • Article 9: Continuous Risk Management System.
  • Article 10: Data Governance & Bias Auditing.
  • Article 14: Human Oversight ('Human-in-the-loop' mandatory).
  • Article 15: Accuracy & Robustness (Quantization warning: INT4 precision degrades mathematical stability under financial audits).
