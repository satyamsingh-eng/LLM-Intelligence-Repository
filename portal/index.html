Production Blockers & Risk Alerts

Warnings discovered during zero-trust engineering audits.

1. DeepSeek 60 RPM Rate Limit Bottleneck

DeepSeek's API is hard-capped at 60 requests per minute. A single multi-agent workflow takes ~21 requests. If 3 users access SARVAX simultaneously, DeepSeek crashes with HTTP 429.
Fix: Use Gemini 2.0 Flash for live UI, and isolate DeepSeek to background cron queues.

2. INT4 Quantization Risk (EU AI Act Article 15)

Super-compressed INT4 models introduce small numeric rounding errors violating EU AI Act Article 15 rules for automated credit scoring. Use FP8 or BF16 precision.
