Production Blockers & Risk Alerts

Critical warnings discovered during zero-trust engineering audits.

1. DeepSeek 60 RPM Rate Limit Bottleneck

DeepSeek's API is hard-capped at 60 requests per minute. A single multi-agent workflow takes ~21 requests. If 3 users use SARVAX at the same time, DeepSeek crashes with HTTP 429.
Fix: Use Gemini 2.0 Flash for live UI, and isolate DeepSeek to background cron queues.

2. INT4 Quantization Warning (EU AI Act Article 15)

Super-compressed INT4 models introduce small numeric rounding errors. Under EU AI Act Article 15 rules, deploying INT4 models for automated credit scoring or financial risk is illegal due to audit inaccuracy. Use FP8 or BF16 precision.
