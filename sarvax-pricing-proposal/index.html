      Six commercial layers.

      Enterprise pricing cannot be reduced to one seat price or one credit wallet. The quote separates recurring platform value, accountable access, multi-agent outcomes, business volume, direct variable costs and implementation.

      Sell outcomes, not bots.

      The 12 catalog agents are grouped into five capability suites. A buyer purchases a governed business capability; Workflow 2.0 decides which agents and controls execute it.

      All 12 catalog agents.

      Each agent is listed separately for scope clarity. None is automatically labeled production-ready; Product and QA approval remains mandatory.

      Every agent tied to real work.

      This map shows where each brochure agent appears in a documented client journey, scoped pilot or evidence-backed discovery. Presence here does not override the Product and QA readiness gate.

      Workflow 2.0 is the system.

      Agents do specialized reasoning. Workflow 2.0 owns the process: order, dependencies, parallel work, connectors, approvals, retries, handoffs, writeback and traceable completion.

      Client pays variable execution directly.

      Credits may exist internally for telemetry and reconciliation, but they are not the enterprise platform price. Direct provider payment is the preferred commercial route where technically feasible.
