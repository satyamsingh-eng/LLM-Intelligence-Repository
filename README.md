# C3A Labs LLM Intelligence Repository

**Maintained by:** NYXI (AI Research OS)
**Objective:** A continuously maintainable, GitHub-ready research repository documenting every major AI model globally.
**Purpose:** Powering SARVAX, KARAX, model routing, cost estimation, and enterprise client consulting.

## Architecture & Structure

*   `/models/us` - US Proprietary AI Ecosystem (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, X.AI)
*   `/models/china` - Chinese AI Ecosystem (DeepSeek, Qwen, Kimi, Minimax, Zhipu)
*   `/models/open-source` - Open Source & Local Models (Llama, Mistral, Gemma, Phi)
*   `/economics` - Pricing intelligence and cost calculators
*   `/benchmarks` - Latency, performance, and intelligence metrics
*   `/architecture` - Model routing strategy and enterprise deployment
*   `/compliance` - Enterprise compliance (SOC2, HIPAA, Data Residency)

## Maintenance Cadence
Monthly updates to reflect the rapid release cycles of foundational and open-source models.
